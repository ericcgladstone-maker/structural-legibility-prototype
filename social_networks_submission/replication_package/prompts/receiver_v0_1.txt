# Receiver Task (v0.1, paper 1)

You are a receiver. You have intercepted a single message that traveled through a communicative production system before reaching you. You must produce a calibrated assessment of three things about the message, plus a fourth judgment when applicable.

(1) **Accuracy** — how likely the message is to faithfully describe the world it claims to describe.
(2) **Production regime** — what kind of production process generated the message.
(3) **Origin** — which source within a known set of candidates originated it.
(4) **Independence** — for regimes involving multiple sources, whether those sources are evidentially independent given the underlying world state.

You will be given (below this task description):
- the intercepted message
- a context packet describing what you know about how the message reached you (this can range from "the message only" to "a full analytic packet")
- a candidate-source set (the universe of possible origins you must consider)

You will produce a single JSON response with all four judgments.

---

## The eight production regimes

Distribute probability across all eight. Exactly one (or one compound of them) produced this message.

1. **single_direct** — one source produced the message; no intermediaries.
2. **chain_relay** — one source's message passed through a sequence of relayers, each transmitting to the next; you have intercepted the terminal of the chain.
3. **independent_corroboration** — multiple sources observed the same underlying event and independently produced related messages.
4. **dependent_repetition** — multiple sources reported, but all of them derive from a single common upstream source. Surface agreement among them does NOT constitute independent evidence.
5. **common_source_laundering** — like dependent repetition, but the common upstream source is concealed behind one or more intermediate sources. Apparent independence is structurally false.
6. **clustered_reinforcement** — content has circulated through a tight community whose members repeat one another's claims; partial dependence determined by community membership.
7. **centralized_synthesis** — multiple inputs were merged by a single synthesizer (a hub-like aggregator) into one output. You see the synthesizer's output.
8. **compound** — two or more of the above regimes occurred sequentially (e.g., laundering followed by chain-relay; clustered reinforcement followed by centralized synthesis).

---

## Probability bins

When stating probabilities, use these calibrated bins (drawn from forecasting practice; Mellers et al. 2014, 2015; Tetlock and Gardner 2015):

- "almost no chance" (0–5%)
- "very unlikely" (5–20%)
- "unlikely" (20–45%)
- "roughly even chance" (45–55%)
- "likely" (55–80%)
- "very likely" (80–95%)
- "almost certain" (95–100%)

You will also report a numerical probability between 0.0 and 1.0.

---

## Confidence-in-judgment (separable from probability)

Rate your **confidence in the judgment itself** — separately from the probability of the event. Use:

- **"low"** — the evidence is weak or contradictory; your judgment could easily be wrong even though you must produce it.
- **"moderate"** — the evidence supports your judgment but with significant uncertainty.
- **"high"** — the evidence clearly supports your judgment.

Probability and confidence are different things. "Very likely / low confidence" (you lean toward yes but aren't sure) is a different state from "roughly even / high confidence" (you are confident that it really could go either way). Report both.

---

## Deferral

For the **origin** question only, you may report "unknown_deferred" if the evidence is genuinely insufficient to commit to any candidate (including "outside_set"). Use this option only when committing would be guessing rather than reasoning. The other judgments (accuracy, regime, independence-when-applicable) must always be answered.

---

## Independence judgment

For regimes that involve multiple sources — regimes 3, 4, 5, 6, and compounds derived from any of these — report the probability that the multiple sources are evidentially independent given the underlying world state. Independent corroboration is rare; surface agreement among sources can arise from common-source structure without being evidentially independent.

For regimes 1, 2, 7, and compounds derived solely from these, set ALL THREE of the following independence_judgment fields exactly as specified:
- `probability_independent`: null
- `confidence_in_judgment`: null
- `reasoning`: "N/A — single-source regime"

---
